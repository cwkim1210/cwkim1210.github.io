Two-Joint Hybrid Soft Finger

Problem
Soft actuators are safe to touch but weak; rigid ones are strong but unsafe around people.
What I did
Fabricated a two-joint finger by fluidic shaping, a process that uses capillary self-organization of liquids under neutral buoyancy, joining 3D printed rigid bones with elastomer joints.
Result
A working actuator that bends at the soft joints while the bones hold their shape, combining compliance and strength.

Heat Flux Sensors for Human Gait

Problem
The lab needed proof that its wearable heat flux sensors were fast and accurate enough for walking trials.
What I did
Proposed and built a PID controlled Peltier cold plate (driving circuit, custom PCB, LabVIEW). Found a confounding variable in conduction based control and pivoted to step input system identification at skin temperature. Set up Vicon motion capture and helped with force plates and human subject trials.
Result
Showed the heat flux sensor responds much faster than the temperature sensor, validating it for gait trials; contributed to a master's thesis on lower limb heat flux.

Autonomous Puck Launching Game

Problem

What I did
Designed a 2.5 DOF Cartesian system with three stepper driven belt linear stages and a servo driven push pull mechanism that grabs, tensions and launches. Wrote a MATLAB vision system with camera calibration to detect and localize pucks, integrated with G-code control.

Fully autonomous retrieval and launch loop from camera to motion.

Critical Fall Height for Leg Fracture

Problem
From what height can a person land on both feet before a leg bone reaches its fracture criterion?
What I did
Modeled the knee as a spring damper two bar linkage and the tibia as an Euler buckling column. A separate estimation study found the minimum velocity for a disk to skip on water at a given impact angle, using dimensional analysis and impulse momentum scaling.
Result
A closed form estimate of critical fall height, written up in LaTeX.
