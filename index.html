P-01

Two-Joint Hybrid Soft Finger

A finger actuator built like a skeleton: rigid 3D printed phalanges connected by soft Ecoflex joints formed with fluidic shaping. Pressurizing the joint chambers bends the finger while the rigid segments hold their shape.

  • Designed rigid bone segments with pin hinge joints
  • Formed silicone joint shells by fluidic shaping
  • Investigated sealing the seam between rigid part and soft joint without adhesives

P-02

Variable Stiffness Gripper with Drop Stitch Fabric

A gripper whose joints can switch between compliant and stiff. Drop stitch fabric, two face sheets tied by thousands of internal threads, becomes rigid when inflated and stays flexible when vented, so it is integrated at the joints as a tunable stiffness element.

  • Sourced and evaluated PVC drop stitch samples (5 cm gap)
  • Developed an edge sealing method: PVC coated polyester strip bonded with vinyl cement

In development

Data Center Waste Heat Recovery

Senior Design capstone (ENGME 460). The team is designing a system that captures waste heat from a hyperscale data center through water to water heat exchange and uses it to warm a treatment chamber where a yeast and water mixture produces gas that is collected.
