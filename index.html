Two-Joint Hybrid Soft Finger

Problem
Soft actuators are safe but weak; rigid ones are strong but unsafe to touch.
What I did
Fabricated a finger from 3D printed rigid bones and elastomer joints made by fluidic shaping.
Result
A working 2 joint actuator that combines compliance and strength.

Problem

What I did
Arduino Nano with dual averaged pulse sensors, visual, audio and haptic alerts, in a rechargeable laser cut case.

Heat Flux Sensors for Human Gait

Problem
The lab needed proof its wearable heat flux sensors were fast and accurate enough for walking trials.
What I did
Built a PID Peltier cold plate (custom PCB, LabVIEW), then designed step input system ID tests at skin temperature.
Result
Showed the sensor responds much faster than the temperature sensor; supported a master's thesis.
