    02Projects

    Builds and their clinical targets

    P-01Completed

    Two-Joint Hybrid Soft Finger

    Rigid 3D printed phalanges joined by Ecoflex joints formed with fluidic shaping. Pressurizing the joint chambers bends the finger while the bones keep their shape.

    Medical relevanceA compliant finger that keeps rigid structure is a building block for soft prosthetic hands.

    EcoflexPLAPneumaticFrumkin Lab
    P-02In development

    Variable Stiffness Gripper

    Drop stitch fabric at the joints: compliant when vented, stiff when inflated. Edges sealed with a PVC coated polyester strip and vinyl cement for airtightness.

    Medical relevanceSwitchable stiffness lets a device stay gentle on contact and firm under load, useful for assistive grasping.

    PVC drop stitch5 cm gapPneumaticFrumkin Lab
    P-03Planned

    Humanoid Soft Robotic Leg

    Antagonistic McKibben artificial muscles, PneuNet ankle actuators and rigid bones with Ecoflex joint shells, sealed so fluid stays in a single joint chamber.

    Medical relevanceA testbed for soft lower limb prostheses and exoskeleton style assistance.

    McKibben PAMPneuNetFluidic shaping
    P-04Planned

    Three-Finger Silicone Gripper

    Ecoflex 00-30 cast in 3D printed molds and driven by a syringe pump, based on the open OptoLAB design (University of Novi Sad).

    Medical relevancePractice in molded silicone actuators, the same process used for soft rehabilitation gloves.

    Ecoflex 00-30Syringe pumpMold design
